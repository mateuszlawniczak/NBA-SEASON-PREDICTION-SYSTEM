NBA Championship Predictor - ArchitectureThis document outlines the technical architecture and logic flow for the NBA Championship Predictor, moving from raw data ingestion to multi-pass Monte Carlo simulations.
1. Data FoundationThe system relies on a robust pipeline to fetch and store historical and real-time performance metrics.Primary Source: nba_api (Fetches raw statistics, box scores, and player tracking data).Storage: Local SQLite database (nba_data.db) for efficient querying and persistence of historical PR trends.
2. Power Rating (PR) EngineThe core analytical component that translates raw box scores into a single numerical value representing a player's or team's "strength."Player PR: A custom mathematical formula calculated for every individual player based on raw stats adjusted by "Specific Effects" (e.g., strength of schedule, defensive matchups).Team PR: Aggregated from individual Player PRs based on projected rotations and expected minute distribution.Formula Logic:$$TeamPR = \sum (PlayerPR \times ProjectedMinutes)
3. Regular Season Simulation (The First Pass)The first simulation layer determines the landscape of the league and identifies post-season contenders.Mechanism: Monte Carlo system (1,000 iterations).Input: Initial Team PRs combined with situational effects such as rest days, travel distance, and home-court advantage.Goal: Generate simulated win/loss records to identify the most likely teams to qualify for the playoffs.
4. Playoff Redefinition (The Second Pass)The system accounts for the fundamental shift in gameplay style that occurs during the post-season.PR Recalculation: For teams that qualify, the Team PR formula is dynamically redefined.The Logic: Playoff basketball differs from the regular season; rotations shorten (star players play more minutes), intensity increases, and specific matchup weights (e.g., star vs. star) carry more significance.Playoff Monte Carlo: A final simulation run utilizing these Playoff-Specific PRs to determine the ultimate Championship outcome.
